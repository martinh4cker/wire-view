WireView 通过 USB 将 Android 平板用作 Apple 芯片 Mac 的有线副屏。Mac 端使用 VideoToolbox 编码，经 ADB reverse 转发；Android 端使用 MediaCodec 和 OpenGL 解码呈现。

- 扩展桌面或镜像屏幕，并保存副屏排列。

# WireView 0.1.0

**Development preview · 2026-11-18**

WireView turns an Android tablet into a wired secondary display for Apple silicon Macs. The Mac encodes with VideoToolbox and forwards over ADB reverse; Android decodes and renders with MediaCodec and OpenGL.

## Downloads

- `WireView-0.1.0-macOS-arm64.zip`: Apple silicon Mac app, ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
- `WireView-0.1.0-Android.apk`: development-signed sideload APK, not a Google Play package.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: SHA-256 checksums for both artifacts.

## Included

- Extended desktop or display mirroring, with saved display placement.
- Per-tablet video settings and USB reconnect.
- Simplified Chinese and English UI, following the device's system language.
- Tested on Apple silicon Mac and one Android 13 tablet.

## Known limitations

The Mac virtual display depends on the private `CGVirtualDisplay` API. A Windows host, touch input, audio, simultaneous streaming to multiple tablets, and stable 90/120 fps are not available. Compatibility beyond the tested devices is unknown.
